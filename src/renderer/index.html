About Zenith

Zenith v2.0.0

A pixel cat desktop companion that sits over your apps and keeps you company — stretch, water & pomodoro reminders, cute idle animations, and a follow-cursor mode that never blocks your clicks.

Repo: github.com/touseef78286/zenith-desktop-pet

Tip: hover to pet, click-drag to move, gear (⚙) for the menu.
